# Couch: how to use it

Couch is the box behind the TV. It plays YouTube and Plex, and music from your phone
(Spotify and AirPlay), and sends the sound to the TV, headphones or a Bluetooth speaker. You
use it with the TV's own remote.

## Turning it on

Turn the TV on and switch to the Couch input (HDMI 1), or just leave Couch plugged in: when
it starts, it turns the TV on by itself. You'll see the Couch logo, "Turning on the TV", then
Home.

## The remote

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Arrows | Move around. Hold one down to keep going. |
| OK | Choose. |
| Hold OK | More options (for example, Forget a Bluetooth speaker). |
| Return | Go back one step. |
| **Hold Return** (about a second) | **Go Home, from anywhere**, even out of YouTube or Plex. |
| Red | Send the sound somewhere else: TV → headphones → Bluetooth → TV. |
| Blue | Quick menu: Home, sound, volume and Bluetooth, on top of whatever's playing. |
| Play, Pause, Stop, ⏪ ⏩ | Control what's playing. |
| Volume + / − / Mute | The TV's volume. When the sound goes to headphones or a Bluetooth speaker, these change their volume instead. |

The remote's own Home button opens the TV's menu, not Couch's. Use **hold Return** instead.

When you turn the TV off, whatever's playing pauses.

## The quick menu

Press Blue, wherever you are, even in the middle of a film. A menu comes up with four
cards: **Home**, **Sound**, **Volume** and **Bluetooth**. Left and Right move between them,
and what's playing underneath carries on and doesn't react.

- **Home**: back to Couch's Home.
- **Sound**: OK sends the sound to the next place, like Red.
- **Volume**: Up and Down make it louder and quieter; OK mutes and unmutes.
- **Bluetooth**: OK opens the Bluetooth screen.

Return or Blue closes the menu. It also closes by itself after half a minute.

## Home

Home is a row of cards: **YouTube**, **Plex**, **Audio**, **Bluetooth** and **Settings**.
Move along them and press OK. The screen takes the colour of the card you're on.

The top right says where the sound is going. To change it, go to **Audio**, press OK and
pick one, or just press Red. While music plays from a phone, it says what's playing instead,
and OK on **Audio** opens Now Playing (Up from Audio still picks the sound).

If nobody touches the remote for 5 minutes, Home dims to a clock. Any button brings Home back
as you left it; that press does nothing else. Nothing dims while a video or music plays.

## YouTube

Choose YouTube on Home. Return on YouTube's own home screen takes you back to Couch's Home.

**Signing in** (needed once): open YouTube and choose **Sign in with your phone**. On your
phone, go to **yt.be/activate** and type in the code from the TV. To watch without an
account, choose **Watch as guest**.

## Plex

Choose Plex on Home to watch your own movies and shows. Plex needs the computer with your
films on it (the Plex server) to be on.

To leave Plex, press Return until it asks "Are you ready to exit Plex?" and choose
**Exit**. Or just hold Return.

**Signing in** (needed once): the first time, Plex shows a code. On your phone, go to
**plex.tv/link** and type it in.

## Bluetooth speakers and headphones

1. Go to **Bluetooth** on Home and press OK.
2. Put the speaker or headphones in pairing mode. That's usually holding its Bluetooth
   button until a light blinks.
3. It appears under **Nearby**. Choose it and press OK.
4. It moves to **Your devices**, and the sound goes to it.

Next time, just turn the speaker on: Couch connects to it by itself within a minute and
the sound goes back to it. When it's off, the sound plays from the TV.

To stop using a speaker, choose it under Your devices, hold OK, and pick **Disconnect** or
**Forget**.

## Music from your phone (Spotify and AirPlay)

Couch is a speaker too. Your phone sees it as **Couch**, as long as the phone is on the same
network (Wi-Fi at home) as Couch.

- **Spotify**: start a song, tap the speaker icon at the bottom of the player, and pick
  **Couch**. This needs Spotify Premium; the free plan can't play to speakers. The first
  time, Couch may take a few seconds to show up.
- **iPhone, iPad or Mac**: open Control Center (on a Mac, the sound menu in the menu bar),
  tap the AirPlay icon, and pick **Couch**. No password.

The music plays wherever Couch's sound goes: the TV, headphones or a Bluetooth speaker. The
TV remote's volume buttons work as usual, and so does the phone's volume.

When music starts, Couch turns the TV on if it's off and shows **Now Playing**: the cover,
the song, the artist and album, and how far it's got, closing YouTube or Plex. Opening
YouTube or Plex while music plays stops the music, and if someone else starts playing from
another phone, the first one stops. (To leave the TV alone instead, turn off Settings ›
Display › **Wake the TV when music starts**: the music then plays behind whatever is on.)

On Now Playing, Left and Right pick a button and OK presses it: previous, Play/Pause and next
for an iPhone or Mac, and **Stop playing on Couch**, which sends the phone away. Couch can't
pause or skip Spotify, so for Spotify there's only Stop: use the phone for the rest. Return
goes Home and the music keeps playing; OK on Audio comes back. When the phone stops playing
to Couch, Now Playing goes back to Home by itself.

On Home and Now Playing, the remote's Play, Pause, ⏪ and ⏩ control the music from an iPhone
or Mac too. To stop, pause on the phone, pick another speaker there, or use Stop playing on
Couch.

Couch doesn't dim to the clock or turn the TV off while music plays, and doesn't update
itself then either.

## Settings

- **Display**: evening colours (softer colours after sunset), how soon Home dims to the
  clock, whether the TV turns itself off after 2 hours with no buttons pressed (off
  unless you turn it on; never while something plays), and whether music from a phone
  wakes the TV and shows Now Playing.
- **Sound**: where the sound goes, a volume limit, whether the TV remote's volume
  buttons work for headphones and Bluetooth, UI sounds: the quiet clicks when you press
  a button on Couch's own screens and in Plex (On, TV only, or Off; YouTube has none), and
  **Spotify and AirPlay**: off, phones don't see Couch as a speaker.
- **Remote**: what the buttons do, and a test that shows what each button sends.
- **Updates**: what version Couch is, and Check now. Once overnight updates are set up
  (a one-time job on a computer), Couch updates itself at night, only when nothing is
  playing, and tells you on Home the next morning.
- **About**: the network and the TV connection.
- **Power**: Restart, and Turn off. Press OK twice; the first press only asks.

## If something looks stuck

1. **Hold Return** for a second. That goes Home from anywhere.
2. Still stuck? Check the TV is on the Couch input (HDMI 1).
3. **"Needs the internet"** or **"Can't reach your Plex server"**: check the internet router and Couch's network cable,
   or that the Plex computer is on, then choose **Try again**.
4. **No sound**: look at the top right of Home. Press Red until it says where you want the
   sound. A Bluetooth speaker has to be on and in range.
5. Nothing on screen reacts? Go to Settings › Power › **Restart**. If you can't get there,
   unplug Couch, wait five seconds and plug it back in. That's safe; it doesn't lose your
   settings or sign-ins.
